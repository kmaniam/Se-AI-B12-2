# ShopEase - Account and Login Support

## Password Reset

1. Open the ShopEase login page.
2. Select Forgot Password.
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Check your inbox for the password reset link.
5. Open the link and create a new password.

## Login Problems

- Verify that the registered email address is correct.
- Check whether Caps Lock is enabled.
- Request a new password reset link if the old link expires.

## Account Security

- Never share your password or OTP.
- ShopEase support will never request your password.
- Suspicious account activity must be reported immediately.
- Suspected account compromise must be escalated to human support.
