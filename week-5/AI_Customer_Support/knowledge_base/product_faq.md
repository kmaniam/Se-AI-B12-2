# ShopEase - Product Frequently Asked Questions

## Product Information

- Product specifications are available on individual product pages.
- Product availability depends on current inventory.
- Prices displayed on the website include applicable taxes.

## Product Replacement

- Customers who receive defective products should contact support
  within 48 hours of delivery.
- Customers should provide photographs and their order ID.
- Support will review the issue before approving a replacement.

## Warranty

- Warranty coverage depends on the product manufacturer.
- Customers should refer to the product page for warranty details.

## Missing Information

- If product information is unavailable in the company documents,
  support must not guess.
- Requests requiring additional investigation should be escalated.
