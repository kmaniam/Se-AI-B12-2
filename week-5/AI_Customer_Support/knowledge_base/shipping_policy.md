# ShopEase - Shipping Policy

## Delivery Timeline

- Standard delivery: 5 to 7 business days.
- Express delivery: 2 to 3 business days.
- Delivery timelines start after order confirmation.
- Customers receive tracking information by email.

## Delayed Deliveries

- Customers can track orders using their order ID.
- If an order is delayed beyond the estimated delivery date,
  customers should contact customer support.
- Support will investigate delayed shipments.
- Delivery delays exceeding 7 business days beyond the
  estimated date must be escalated to human support.

## Shipping Charges

- Standard shipping is free for orders above Rs. 999.
- Orders below Rs. 999 have a Rs. 50 shipping fee.
- Express shipping costs Rs. 150.
