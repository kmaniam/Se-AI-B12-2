# ShopEase - Refund and Return Policy

## Return Eligibility

- Customers can request a return within 30 days of delivery.
- Products must be unused and in original packaging.
- Certain personal care and hygiene products cannot be returned.

## Refund Processing

- Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.
- Refunds are credited to the original payment method.
- Customers receive an email confirmation after approval.

## Damaged Products

- Customers should report damaged products within 48 hours
  of delivery.
- Customers should provide photographs of the damaged item.
- Support will review the request and arrange a replacement
  or refund according to the investigation.

## Important

- Refund approval is subject to eligibility checks.
- Payment disputes must be escalated to human support.
